Add Hybrid Coach to your iPhone Home Screen

  1. Open the hosted Hybrid Coach link in Safari.
  2. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App.
  5. Tap Add.

Once installed, it opens from its own Home Screen icon and can work offline after the first successful load.
